# Every Day

The daily routine. About 15 minutes total, spread across the day.

The week's posts are already written and scheduled from Sunday. This sheet is
everything else, and it is the part that actually grows the page.

These are things that should happen each day, not one person's job. Hand out
whatever makes sense for who is working.

---

## Morning: share the 11am post to Facebook groups

**This is the highest-value 10 minutes of the day.** Our page reaches the
people who already follow us. Groups reach everyone else.

**Share from a personal account, not the business page.** Most local groups
ban businesses from promoting themselves. A person sharing something happening
locally is welcome. The business page posting the same thing gets deleted or
gets you banned.

Anyone on staff with a personal Facebook can do this. More people sharing to
different groups beats one person sharing to all of them.

### How to do it right

1. Open the 11am post on the Backyard Brew page
2. Hit Share, choose a group
3. **Write a real sentence above it.** Never paste the marketing caption
4. Post
5. Wait a few minutes before the next group
6. Check back later and answer anyone who replies

### The line you write above it matters most

Write like a person who happens to know about it. Not like an ad.

**Good:**

> Pickleball tournament out at Backyard Brew tonight if anyone's looking for
> something to do. Free to play.
>
> They do 9 rounds of bingo Mondays out on Sand Acres. Kids can play too.

**Bad:**

> Join us tonight at Backyard Brew for an unforgettable evening! 🍻🎉

## All day: stories

Stories are where people see that the place is alive right now. Posts are
planned. Stories are real.

**Never fewer than 2 a day. Past that, more is always better.** There is no
upper limit and no such thing as too many. On a big event night, a packed
Thursday market, live music, a tournament, shoot everything and post it all.
Those are the nights that pull new people in.

Do not watch the clock. Post at these moments instead, because they happen on
their own:

| Moment | What to shoot |
|---|---|
| **1. Doors open** | Setting up, the empty patio, the course, taps ready |
| **2. Food comes out** | Any plate leaving the kitchen. Tacos, pizza, nachos |
| **3. The event starts** | Bingo cards out, paddles going, first song |
| **4. It's going** | The crowd, a winner, the music, people on the course |
| **5. Closing out** | Last call, the sunset, the fire |

Hit 2 of those and the day counts. Hit all 5 and it was a good day. On a busy
night, keep going past 5.

**Two easy ones people forget:**

- Reshare the day's feed post to your story. Five seconds, real reach
- Reshare any story a customer tags us in. Costs nothing, and they see it

**Do not force anyone on camera.** Some staff would rather not, and that is
fine. Nobody has to be in a story to work here.

**Phone stays vertical.** Always.

## First hour after each post: reply to comments

The first hour matters most. Comments early tell Facebook and Instagram to
show the post to more people.

- Reply to every comment, even "🔥" or "nice." A short reply counts
- Answer questions with the real answer. Hours, prices, dogs, whether you need
  your own discs
- Do not know? Ask Claude, or check `1-START-HERE`

**Something rude or a complaint?** Do not answer it right away. Use `/reply`
in the Claude app. Paste in the comment and say whatever you actually want to
say. It writes the version that is safe to post.

## While you are there: take photos

Every photo taken today is a post next week. When there is nothing to shoot,
Sunday gets harder.

Drop them in the `photos/` folder and name them with the date and event, like
`2026-09-14_bingo.jpg`.

Worth grabbing:

- Anything busy
- Food, always
- The course and the trails in good light
- Winners, regulars, dogs
- The taps
