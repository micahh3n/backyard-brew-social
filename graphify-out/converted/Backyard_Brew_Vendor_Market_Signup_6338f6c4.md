## Sheet: Copy-Paste Messages
| BACKYARD BREW — COPY-PASTE VENDOR MESSAGES |
| --- |
| Common messages for vendor signups. Copy the one you need, swap the bracketed [DATE] / [DAY] / [DUSK TIME] placeholders, and paste it into your reply. |
| 1. THE RUNDOWN — send when someone asks for more info or to be in the market |
| Here's the rundown:

You bring: Your own tent, table, and setup. There's no fee, no commission, and you keep 100% of your sales.

We ask: Help us promote the event on your end (a post, a story, whatever's easiest for you) and grab something from the bar while you're setting up. We're a small business too, and it helps us keep hosting stuff like this. We also ask that all vendors handle their own trash so the course and property stay clean.

Spots are first come, first served. The event starts at 4pm, so I'd recommend coming early to set up beforehand. We don't allow setup after 4pm, so please be ready to go before the event starts.

Food vendors: You'll need a Village of Ashwaubenon Food Vendor License to serve at our market.

I just need a couple things from you:
- Name of your business
- What you sell |
| 2. POLITE DECLINE — similar vendor already booked for this event |
| It sounds like you sell some really cool stuff, but we already have a couple people signed up with similar products for this event, and we're trying to keep a good variety so everyone gets a fair shot at sales.

I can't add you to this one, but I'll keep you in mind for future events. We're hoping to make this a regular thing, so feel free to check back closer to the date in case a spot opens up. And you're always welcome to come out and check out the market anyway!

Thank you! |
| 3. VENDOR SPOTS FULL — waitlist reply |
| Thanks so much for reaching out about Backyard Market & Brews! We're all filled up on vendor spots for [DATE]'s market. Spots are first come, first served, and they went quick!

That said, I'll save your info. If this keeps going the way we're hoping, we want to make it a regular thing, so you're on our radar for the next one. Keep an eye on our Facebook and Instagram for posts about vendor openings and upcoming markets. That's where we'll announce it first.

In the meantime, come hang out at the market anyway! [DATE], 4pm til dusk. Grab a beer, check out the other vendors, and get a feel for the whole thing. Would love to see you there even if you're not set up this round. |
| 4. CONFIRMED VENDOR INFO SHEET — send a few days before the event |
| Attention all vendors!

We've officially filled all our vendor slots for Backyard Market & Brews on [DAY], [DATE], starting at 4pm. Thank you all so much for being part of it! If anything comes up and you don't think you'll be able to make it, please message or email us as soon as possible so we can offer your spot to someone on our waiting list.

Here's everything you need to know for the day:

Parking
- After you unload, vendor parking is along Highland Ridge Drive (north side of the bar).
- Please don't leave vehicles in our lot after unloading. We want to keep that clear for customers.

Check-In & Setup
- Earliest arrival/check-in/setup time: 1pm
- Latest setup time: 3:45pm (no setups after this)
- When you arrive, check in at the bar first and grab something from the bar before setting up your booth
- You'll receive your market space assignment at check-in
- Spaces are roughly 12' x 12' so please plan your tent/table setup accordingly
- No outside food or drink allowed on the property. Reusable water bottles are fine outside the bar.

Electricity
- No electricity will be provided to vendors, so please plan accordingly.

Takedown
- Please hold off on packing up until the band stops playing, around [DUSK TIME]/dusk.
- Takedown will start with the tents closest to the parking lot first so we can keep things moving in an orderly fashion. Please wait for your turn.
- Make sure to take all of your trash with you when you leave to help keep everything clean.

Cleanup
- All vendors are responsible for cleaning up trash at their own booth/area.

Promotion
- Help us make this thing huge! Please promote the event on your own social pages. The more buzz, the better for all of us.

A quick disclaimer: Backyard Brew is not responsible for weather-related cancellations, or for any damage to or theft of vendor property during the event. In the event of bad weather, it's up to each vendor to decide whether to stay and wait out the storm or leave. By checking in on [DATE], you're acknowledging these terms and the risks involved.

Thank you again for being part of this! Let's make it a great one. Reach out with any questions. |
